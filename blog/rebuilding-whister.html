Rebuilding Whister from the Ground Up

We want to be clear about the browser download currently available on the Whister website: it is an early experimental build. It represents an earlier stage of the project, not the finished version of Whister that we want to deliver.

We’ve decided to start the browser again from a clean foundation. Building a browser involves far more than its visible interface, and the early build has taught us a lot about the complexity involved. A fresh start gives us room to organize the project more carefully and make it easier to test and maintain as development continues.

What this means right now

The existing download is still an early build and may be incomplete, unstable, or change significantly. If you try it, please treat it as experimental rather than a polished daily-use browser.

The rebuild is in progress. We’ll share more as there is meaningful progress to show. We don’t have a release date to announce yet, and we’d rather wait until the new build is ready than promise a date we can’t stand behind.

Thank you for following along

Thanks to everyone who has checked out Whister, shared feedback, or followed the project so far. The early version helped us understand what needs to improve. We’re taking those lessons into the rebuild and will keep posting updates here.
