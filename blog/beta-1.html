Whister Beta 1 Is Almost Here

When we started rebuilding Whister, we said we would share more once there was something real to show. There now is. Beta 1 of the rebuilt browser is working, and we are finishing testing before the download goes live. It is not released yet, and the current download on the site is still the early experimental build.

What is in Beta 1

  • A native new tab page. Built into the browser itself, with a search box that lets you choose your own search engine, and local weather.
  • Whister settings. A settings page designed for Whister, with separate pages for search, appearance, startup, privacy, autofill and languages. Pick your search engine, theme, text size, home page and more.
  • A first-run setup screen. A short welcome that asks for your search engine, privacy choices and startup behaviour, so Whister feels like yours from the first launch.
  • Whister branding. New logo throughout, including the version page, and a dark toolbar theme.
  • A Windows installer. A simple installer that does not need administrator rights and adds a Start menu entry, a desktop shortcut and a proper uninstaller.

Known issues

This is a beta, and we would rather be upfront about it:

  • The new tab page can occasionally crash. We are tracking this down.
  • Whister does not update itself yet. For now you will need to download new builds from this site.
  • Some pages, such as history, extensions and the password manager, still use the standard browser design. We plan to replace them over time.
  • Windows only for now.

What happens next

We are working through the last two milestones: finishing the installer and stability testing. The progress bar on the home page shows where we are. When Beta 1 is ready, we will announce it here and on the home page. Thank you for following along and for all the feedback so far.
