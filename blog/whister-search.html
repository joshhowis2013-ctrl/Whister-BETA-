Whister Search is here 🚀🚀

Whister Search is here designed To Search without trackers. Its baseed on brave search and aims to give you a fast and secure browsering experence

What makes whister search special?

You can run it locally

  • Whister Search runs entirely on your local machine, ensuring your search queries remain private.
  • No data is sent to external servers, giving you full control over your search experience.

Privacy-focused

Whister Search is built with privacy in mind. It does not track your searches or store personal information.

Your search history remains on your device, giving you full control over your data.

By running locally, it minimizes exposure to third-party trackers and ads.

Enjoy a secure and private search experience without compromising on speed or functionality.
