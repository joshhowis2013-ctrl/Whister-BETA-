Whister 2.0 Beta 2 Is Here

Whister 2.0 Beta 2 is now available for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). This is the second public build of the rebuilt browser. It is an early pre-release: expect rough edges, back up anything important, and test it in a virtual machine before considering regular use.

What is in Beta 2

  • Bug fixes and improvements. Various stability and performance enhancements over Beta 1.
  • Updated installer. The installation process has been refined for a smoother setup experience.
  • Performance improvements. Optimizations have been made to enhance browsing speed and responsiveness.
  • Security updates. Important security fixes have been applied to protect against known vulnerabilities.
  • Meet whister blocker Whister now includes a built-in content blocker to enhance your browsing privacy and security.

Whister Blocker

Whister Blocker is our built-in content blocker designed to enhance your browsing privacy and security. It helps prevent unwanted ads, trackers, and other intrusive content from affecting your browsing experience.

You can configure Whister Blocker in the browser settings, where you can enable or disable it, manage blocked content, and customize your privacy preferences.

Whister blocker blocks sites, trackers, ads, and other unwanted content to provide a cleaner and safer browsing experience.

Its not perfect yet, and we are continuously working to improve its effectiveness and compatibility with various websites. Your feedback is valuable in helping us make Whister Blocker better.

Known issues

This is a pre-release, and we want to be clear about its current limits:

  • Whister blocker blocks may not work perfectly on all websites yet.
  • The first-run welcome may still show Chromium’s stock screen in this build.
  • The settings for this whister blocker may sometimes reset In the FULL settings.
  • Ongoing improvements are being made to enhance compatibility with various websites.
  • Some browser pages still use Chromium’s standard interface.
  • Whister does not update itself; download future builds manually from the GitHub release page.
  • Windows only for now.
