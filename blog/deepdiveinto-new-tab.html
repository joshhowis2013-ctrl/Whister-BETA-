Deep-Dive into The New Tab

In this deep dive, we explore the features and customization options available in the new tab of Whister 2.0 Beta 1.

Introduction

The new tab in Whister 2.0 Beta 1 Is Engineered to be distraction-free while providing quick access to essential tools and information.

Whister New Tab Features

  • Search bar: Quickly search the web from the new tab.
  • Time and date: Displays the current time and date prominently.
  • Appearance customization: Choose themes, background images, and layout options.
  • Selectable search engine: Easily switch between different search engines.

These features are designed to make the new tab both functional and customizable, providing quick access to search, time, and personalization options.

How to Use the New Tab

To make the most of the new tab, start by customizing its appearance and selecting your preferred search engine. Use the search bar for quick web searches and keep an eye on the time and date for better productivity.

We hope you enjoy the new tab and find it a useful addition to your browsing experience.
