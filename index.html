Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our modern privacy web browser architecture.

Is Whister completely free to use?

Yes! Our browser is open-source and entirely free to download and use. We never track your behavior, display advertising layers, or sell your search matrices to advertising networks.

Can I import my data from Chrome or Brave?

Absolutely. During your initial startup installation wizard, you can port over all active cache configurations, saved profiles, credentials, bookmarks, and extensions with a single click.

How does the built-in Bang engine work?

By entering a prefix parameter keyword sequence like !yt or !gh inside your search input bar field, the internal system bypasses generic indices and opens up deep search landing layouts safely inside new windows.
